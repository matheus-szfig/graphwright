# D-10 · Goldens are PageStates checked into tests, generated from demo-site

*2026-10-25 · implemented*

## Context and decision

`engine/tests/fixtures/goldens/` holds observed PageStates of demo-site pages (D-29),
regenerated by a script, so engine tests resolve targets, recognize and evaluate
transitions offline and reproducibly. Goldens from real third-party sites belong in the
consuming project, not here — they would pin this repo to someone else's markup.
